# MC630 PLT Studio v0.1.9

- Windows-style theme and system icons, compact toolbars, and separate Registration tab.
- Refined Print dialog, printer controls, and background print preparation.
- On-canvas Crop tool with eight handles, thirds grid, Apply/Reset/Cancel, and undo.
- Faster Pen previews, nib cursor, Shift angle constraints, and Alt-click to remove the outgoing handle while preserving the incoming curve.
- Straight rectangular cut option, smaller sliders, and revised zoom controls.
- Oversized single images remain visible at their original scale in the editor; print/cut page validation remains in place.
- Manual alignment crosses are hidden on the canvas by default; guides can be deleted by dragging outside the page.
